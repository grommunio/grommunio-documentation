**grommunio Archive** keeps a tamper-proof copy of all mail that is sent and received, for legal and compliance reasons and as a safety net. With the **Archive** plugin you can search it directly in grommunio Web, even for mails that have long been deleted from your mailbox.

## Switching Archive on

1. Open **Settings › Plugins**.
2. Tick **Archive** and click **Apply**.
3. Reload grommunio Web when asked.

An **Archive** tab appears in the top bar. Your administrator may also have it open automatically after you sign in.

## Searching the archive

Click **Archive** in the top bar. grommunio Archive opens in a grommunio Web tab:

![grommunio Archive inside grommunio Web with the search field, the result list and the preview pane](/img/web/web_archive.png)

1. Enter your search terms, for example a name, an address or words from the subject, and click **Search**.
2. Use **Advanced search** to search by sender, recipient, subject, date, attachments or tags.
3. Click a mail in the result list to show it in the preview pane.

With the buttons below the list you can restore a selected mail to your mailbox, download it as a file or add tags to it.

:::note
What you find in the archive depends on what your organization archives and how long it is kept. You only see the mails you were involved in, unless your administrator gave you auditor rights.
:::
