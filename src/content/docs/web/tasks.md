Tasks help you keep track of what needs to be done, and by when. The **To-Do List** collects your tasks and every mail you have [flagged for follow-up](/web/mail/#flags-and-follow-up), so nothing gets lost.

![The To-Do List with tasks sorted by due date, their categories and follow-up flags](/img/web/web_tasks_list.png)

## The To-Do List

The folder pane shows two lists:

- **To-Do List**: your tasks *and* the mails and contacts you flagged, in one list.
- **Tasks**: only your tasks.

Each task shows a checkbox, the subject, the due date, its categories and a flag. Overdue tasks are shown in red, completed tasks are crossed out.

### Filtering

Use the buttons above the list to show only some of your tasks:

![The filter buttons Active, Upcoming, Complete and Overdue](/img/web/web_tasks_filters.png)

| Filter | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Active** | tasks that are not completed |
| **Upcoming** | tasks that are due in the next days |
| **Complete** | completed tasks |
| **Overdue** | tasks whose due date has passed |

Click the active filter again to show all tasks.

### Quick actions

- Tick the **checkbox** in front of a task to mark it as complete, untick it to reopen it.
- Hover over a task for **Mark Complete** and **Delete**.
- Right-click a task for all actions:

![The context menu of a task with Open, Follow up, Copy/Move, Categories, Delete and Options](/img/web/web_tasks_contextmenu.png)

## Creating a task

1. Click **New** in the toolbar, or choose **New** › **Task** from any view.
2. Enter a **Subject** and, if you like, a start and due date.
3. Click **Save & Close**.

You can also turn a mail into a task: right-click the mail and choose **Create task**. The text of the mail is copied into the task.

![The task Send banner files to BrightPixel with start and due date, status In Progress, priority High, 50 % complete and a checklist in the notes](/img/web/web_task_dialog.png)

### The Task tab

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | What needs to be done. |
| **Start date / Due date** | When work starts and when it must be finished. |
| **Status** | Not Started, In Progress, Complete, Wait for other person or Deferred. |
| **Priority** | Low, Normal or High. |
| **% Complete** | How far the work has progressed. 100 % marks the task as completed. |
| **Reminder** | Date and time at which grommunio Web reminds you. |
| **Notes** | A description, checklist or anything else. |
| **Attachments** | Files belonging to the task. |

The toolbar offers **Save & Close**, **Delete**, **Add attachment**, **Mark Complete**, **Print**, **Assign Task**, **Recurrence**, **Categories**, **Private** and **Follow up**.

### The Details tab

![The Details tab with date completed, total and actual work, mileage, billing information and companies](/img/web/web_task_details.png)

The **Details** tab records the effort: **Date completed**, **Total work** and **Actual work** (in hours, days or weeks), **Mileage**, **Billing information** and **Companies**. These fields are optional.

### Recurring tasks

Click **Recurrence** to repeat a task, for example *every month on the 1st*. When you complete one occurrence, the next one is created automatically.

## Assigning a task to someone else

You can ask a colleague to take over a task. This is called a *task request*.

1. Choose **New** › **Task request**, or open a task and click **Assign Task**.
2. Enter the colleague in **To**.
3. Fill in the task as usual. Leave **Track progress** ticked to keep an updated copy of the task and to receive status reports automatically.
4. Click **Send**.

![A task request to Maria Rossi: Prepare print versions of the key visuals](/img/web/web_task_request.png)

Your colleague receives the request in their Inbox and can **Accept** or **Decline** it:

![The task request in the inbox of Maria Rossi with the Accept and Decline buttons](/img/web/web_task_request_received.png)

![The dialog to send the response now or edit it first](/img/web/web_task_request_accept.png)

Once accepted, the task belongs to your colleague. You keep a copy in your list that is updated whenever they change the status or the percentage complete:

![The To-Do List of Anna Berger with the assigned task Prepare print versions of the key visuals](/img/web/web_task_assigned.png)

:::tip
If you assigned a task and want to work on it yourself as well, open your copy and click **Create Unassigned Copy** on the Details tab.
:::
