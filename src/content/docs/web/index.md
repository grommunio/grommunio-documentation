grommunio Web is the browser interface of grommunio. It brings your e-mail, calendar, contacts, tasks and notes together with files, chat and video meetings, and it works in any modern browser. There is nothing to install.

![grommunio Web showing the inbox of Anna Berger with the reading pane open on a partner proposal](/img/web/web_overview.png)

## Who this manual is for

This manual is written for the people who use grommunio Web every day. It does not assume any previous knowledge. It explains what you see on the screen, what each button does and how typical tasks are done, from writing your first mail to booking a meeting room or letting the AI assistant summarize a long thread.

The examples follow **Anna Berger**, Marketing Manager at the fictional company *Example Ltd.*, through a normal working week: a product launch called *Aurora*, a trade fair, a website relaunch and a lot of mail.

:::note
Your administrator decides which features and plugins are available. If a button or a settings page described here is missing in your grommunio Web, it has probably been switched off for your account.
:::

## What's new in grommunio Web 5

grommunio Web 5 is the largest update in the history of the web client. The highlights:

| Area | What changed |
|---|---|
| **Look and feel** | A redesigned interface with larger controls, a new sign-in page and a **dark mode** for the whole client, switchable from the top bar. |
| **Keyboard** | A **command palette** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) to jump to any folder, view or setting, and **undo/redo** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Y</kbd>) for deleting, moving, flagging and more. |
| **Document viewer** | PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OpenDocument, images, audio, video and attached mails open directly in the browser. |
| **Attachments** | Select several attachments, drag them to another message or to your desktop as a ZIP file, save them to a folder or remove them from a stored message. |
| **Security** | **OpenPGP** next to S/MIME, with one shared pair of *Sign* and *Encrypt* buttons. |
| **Works like Outlook** | Categories, safe senders and blocked senders are stored the way Outlook stores them, so they are the same in grommunio Web, Outlook and on your phone. |
| **AI Assistant** | Summarize and translate mails, let the assistant suggest meetings, tasks and replies, and improve your own texts (if your administrator enabled it). |
| **Mail** | Conversation view also in shared mailboxes, sender logos (BIMI), sticky notes on mails and folder suggestions when moving mail. |
| **Profile** | Profile pictures from a file, Gravatar or Libravatar, with a crop dialog. |
